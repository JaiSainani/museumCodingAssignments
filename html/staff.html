Dr. Isla Raman

Subject Matter Expertise: Archaeologist — Digital Stratigrapher

Professional Bio

Dr. Raman earned her PhD focusing on ritual highland sites. She combines careful excavation with photogrammetry, LIDAR, and ceramic analysis to model layers without disturbing fragile contexts. Her open data reports, reproducible field logs, and clear diagrams make results easy to audit.

Museum Role

Leads the “Echoes in Stone” gallery and designs the interactive excavation map that visitors can explore.

Special Considerations

  • Multiple fieldwork fellowships; open-access site reports.
  • Fluent in English/Hindi; GIS and R catalog analytics.
  • Advocates transparent provenance and community review.

Dr. Raj Malik

Subject Matter Expertise: Physical Anthropologist — Osteology & Isotope Analysis

Professional Bio

Dr. Malik studies human skeletal biology, mortuary practice, and biogeochemical pathways using osteometry, micro-CT, and stable isotopes (δ¹³C/δ¹⁵N/Sr). He emphasizes contamination control, clear chain of custody, and accessible reporting. He mentors interns in respectful handling of human remains and collaborates with descendant communities.

Museum Role

Directs the Osteology Lab and leads the “Bone Stories” demo on inferring age, sex, activity, and health from bone.

Special Considerations

  • Repatriation casework and community consultations.
  • Trauma-pattern training modules for staff.
  • Publishes replication datasets with careful anonymization.

Dr. Francine Bowman

Interpretive Discipline: Symbolic Analyst — Semiotics & Narratives

Professional Bio

Dr. Bowman triangulates material evidence with oral histories and multilingual inscriptions to build balanced, readable narratives. Her work centers socio-political context and descendant voices. She turns complex ideas into approachable exhibit text and interactive media without losing rigor.

Interpretive Method

Cross-references inscriptions with motif databases, interviews cultural memory holders, compares parallel myths, and prototypes immersive scenes with clearly labeled conjecture.

Special Considerations

  • Decolonial museology; clear labeling of provenance and uncertainty.
  • Bilingual exhibit author (English/Spanish) with accessibility-first writing.
  • Facilitates roundtables to resolve interpretive clashes.
